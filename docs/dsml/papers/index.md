# Past papers

Two mid-semester and two end-semester papers, every Units 1–4 question solved in the layout you would write in the exam. Every number in the solutions comes from running a script, not from typing. The [mock paper](../mock-mst) is a third mid-semester in the same shape.

<SourceTable kinds="mst,endsem,mock" />

::: tip Which papers count most
1. **[September 2025](./mst-2025)**: last year's mid-semester for this course. Do it first, timed (skip Q4, which is Unit 5).
2. **[Spring 2025](./mst-2025-spring)**: the sibling course. Its Q5a and Q5b came back word for word in September 2025.
3. **[Autumn 2024 end-semester](./endsem-2024)**: your course's own end-semester. Its Units 1–4 questions are longer versions of mid-semester ones: Pearson r, z-score outliers, ridge, a decision tree.
4. **[Spring 2025 end-semester](./endsem-2025)**: short 5-mark calculations, good for speed practice.
:::

## How the mid-semester is set {#pattern}

Two papers, 55 marks between them, five questions each, **no choice**:

| Every paper so far had… | Typical marks | Where to learn it |
|---|---|---|
| A **gradient descent** calculation: one or two iterations by hand | 5–6 | [Unit 4A](../notes/unit-4a#gradient-descent) |
| A **scaling and encoding** theory question ("prefer min-max over standardization…", "two issues of one-hot…"), word for word in both | 4–6 | [Unit 2](../notes/unit-2#scaling) |
| An **evaluation** calculation: confusion-matrix metrics or an ROC table | 5–6 | [Unit 3](../notes/unit-3#confusion-matrix) |
| A **classifier** question: KNN off a plot, or a logistic-regression probability | 5–6 | [Unit 4B](../notes/unit-4b#knn), [Unit 4A](../notes/unit-4a#logistic) |
| Something from Unit 5 (k-means, PCA) | 2–6 | Not in your syllabus this year |

Both papers are about 80% calculation. The theory parts are short and always phrased as "when would you prefer…", "assess two issues…" or "analyse the impact of…". [How to answer](../how-to-answer) has a template for each. [How often each topic repeats](../repeats) has the full map, including the end-semester papers.

::: tip Attempting a paper
Hide the solutions (the default), set a 90-minute timer, and write on paper with a calculator. Then open the solutions and mark yourself honestly. Tick **Mark done** only when you could produce the answer without looking.
:::
