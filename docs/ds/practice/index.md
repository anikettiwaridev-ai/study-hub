# Practice sheets

The three practice sheets from class, 30 questions, every one solved with a program that compiles and runs. Many solutions are the same programs the notes use, so the sheets and the notes reinforce each other.

<SourceTable kinds="assignment" />

::: tip Where the sheets meet the exams
- Sheet 2 Q10 (**polynomial addition, sparse matrix as a list**) became [Oct 2025 Q6](../papers/mst-2025-oct#q6) (sparse-matrix addition).
- Sheet 2 Q9 and Sheet 3 Q1–Q2 (**linked stack**) are [Mar 2024 Q4](../papers/mst-2024-mar#q4) and the base of [Dec 2023 Q2](../papers/endsem-2023#q2).
- Sheet 3 Q4–Q5 (**conversions and evaluation**) are on every mid-semester paper.
- Sheet 3 Q6 (**circular queue**, telling full from empty) is [Oct 2025 Q3](../papers/mst-2025-oct#q3) in reverse.
- Sheet 3 Q10 (**Round Robin with a queue**) is the shape of [2024 B Q2](../papers/mst-2024-b#q2) (`candy_crush`).
:::
