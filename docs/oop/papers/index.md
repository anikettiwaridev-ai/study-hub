# Past papers

The four most recent mid-semester papers, the questions from the 2024 end-semester paper that fall inside the mid-semester syllabus, and this semester's Quiz 1. After these, try the [mock paper](../mock-mst).

<SourceTable kinds="mst,endsem,quiz,mock" />

::: tip Attempting a paper
Keep solutions hidden and give yourself the full 1.5 hours. Write on paper, then compare. Mark a question done only when you could answer it without looking.
:::

::: info Which paper is which
October 2025 is the regular paper from the same point in the course as you are now. November 2025 is its re-sit (remedial) and the newest paper we have. September and October 2024 are the previous year's paper and its re-sit; the re-sit copies the original question for question with new data, which is the clearest sign of how much she recycles.
:::
